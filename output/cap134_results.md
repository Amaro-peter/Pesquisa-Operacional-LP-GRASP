## 3. What this instance can and cannot show

- On **quality** the two randomized arms are indistinguishable (0.0000% against 0.0000%).
- On **time** the LP-biased arm is **2.80× faster** (52 ms against 144 ms), of which 16 ms is the LP solve it alone pays for.
- B found its best at restart 1 of 32; C at restart 1 of 32.

Because the relaxation is integral, this instance cannot discriminate between the
methods on quality — only on cost. It is reported for exactly that reason: a
comparison that showed a large quality difference here would indicate a problem with
the comparison, not a property of the methods.
